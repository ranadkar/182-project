# Notes for the December 7 revision

Unmodified Qwen executed a tool call on each of 108 external discrepancies. Sixty responses were read-only and 34 reached the suggested state. The clearest training comparison pairs the same cases across arms. Continuation and explicit training differed by −0.3 percentage points (95% interval −3.7 to +2.8) on unsolicited target changes and by +37.7 points (+26.2 to +48.1) on authorized continuation. The latter gap largely reflects the decline from 63.0% accuracy in unmodified Qwen to 24.1% after explicit training. [Trial records](../qwen/v1/results/trials.csv) · [Independent estimates](../qwen/v1/results/estimates.csv)

**Controls for the next draft.** Add a non-tool SFT condition matched for budget and domains to test whether the common increase across adapters is specific to supervised tool practice. For delivery, Figure 3 shows user, external and system rates for identical observations. Crossing source attribution with serialization while holding content constant would help distinguish conversational expectations from sensitivity to the wrapper. [Saved prompts](../qwen/v1/data/evaluation_prompts.jsonl)

**Coverage and uncertainty.** Eighteen synthetic domains include recurring operation types. Three fitting seeds are few for estimating small treatment effects. Secondary intervals are unadjusted. For V2, have an independent author assign evidence and authorization labels before inspecting outputs. Add fitting seeds if small arm differences will be interpreted. Freeze the protocol before opening the twelve December holdback cases. [Design and split](../qwen/v1/design.md)

**Initiation and tool interfaces.** A scheduled generation after an external message supplies an opportunity to act. Header choice strongly affects the response. A first read might precede a write, and a correct ledger update gives no evidence about a model’s beliefs concerning permission. A useful extension is to follow reads with a deterministic simulator result and score the second decision, preserving separate counts for inspection, redundant writes, net changes and target matches. Keep the first-turn measure for comparability. The public-data audit has one annotator, a small bounded sample, and no task-free opening from which to estimate a conditional action rate.
