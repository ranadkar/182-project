# Expanded and matched evaluation

**The overcalling–undercalling trade-off survives the expansion, and the protocol gap survives exact question matching.** The additional analysis covers the complete released When2Call judge set: 300 distinct prompts in 272 source-question clusters, with 100 tool-required, 100 clarification and 100 inability cases. Every prompt matched all four continuations in its original MCQ record. The same frozen NF4 base and eight adapters ran both protocols. The 72 previously inspected generations and 27 matching MCQs per model were reused. The expanded test includes those 72. [Input and checkpoint audit](results/expanded-generation/audit.json)

| Non-tool training | False calls on 200 non-tool requests | Generated calls on 100 required requests | Exact generated calls on 100 required requests | MCQ tool selections on the same 100 required requests |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| NF4 base | 47.5% | 99.0% | 61.0% | 100.0% |
| 0% | 91.5% | 100.0% | 64.5% | 100.0% |
| 10% | 37.8% | 96.0% | 64.5% | 100.0% |
| 25% | 18.8% | 83.0% | 58.5% | 100.0% |
| 50% | 10.0% | 67.0% | 50.5% | 99.0% |

Each mixture averages seeds 182 and 183. From 25% to 50%, false calls fell by **8.8 percentage points** [5.6, 12.3], required-call recall by **16.0** [10.1, 22.5], and exact-call recall by **8.0** [3.9, 12.9]. The tool-only adapters called on all 183 non-tool cases that offered any tool. On the 228 additional prompts alone, the same ordering of false calls and required recall held. The 25% mixture's exact-recall difference from the base was −2.5 points [−9.5, +5.0]. That interval allows both a loss and a gain. [Generation counts and paired intervals](results/expanded-generation/tables.md) · [Trade-off figure](figures/expanded-generation/tradeoff.pdf)

**Candidate scoring changes both sorts of error.** On identical questions the balanced adapters selected a supplied tool call 32.0 points more often than they generated a call on required requests [23.0, 40.7]. On non-tool requests they selected a call 47.0% of the time compared with 10.0% in generation, a paired increase of 37.0 points [29.4, 44.2]. Overall four-class accuracy was 65.5% under MCQ and 74.0% under generation. For seeds 182 and 183 respectively, 36 and 29 required cases were MCQ-only calls; zero and one were generation-only calls. The gap concerns ranking a provided action versus producing one. It does not establish an internal mechanism or make selecting the candidate equivalent to generating its arguments. [Full matched counts](results/matched-protocols/tables.md) · [Paired figure](figures/matched-protocols/paired-protocols.pdf)

**Restraint did not transfer to directly answerable lookup questions in the authored control.** We froze 36 families with three variants per family and an identical two-tool menu within each family. The base called on 20/36 directly answerable requests. The 0%, 10% and 25% adapters called on all 36 in both seeds. The balanced adapters called on 35/36 and 36/36, or **98.6%**, 43.1 points more often than the base [27.8, 59.7]. The one exception asked for an identifier already present. Both balanced adapters correctly sought clarification on 36/36 underspecified variants. They made exact calls on 35/36 and 36/36 paired required lookups. On this design the adapters distinguished missing identifiers from present ones much more reliably than they distinguished an unknown fact from a supplied fact. [Family counts and confusions](results/direct-answer-controls/tables.md) · [Control figure](figures/direct-answer-controls/paired-direct-controls.pdf)

**What remains inconclusive.** The official data have no gold direct answers. The authored families share a two-tool lookup structure, and the training mixtures have no labeled direct targets. These observations cannot identify which training feature produced the direct-answer failure or how common it is elsewhere. Four official required references violate their advertised schema. Local text-act classification remains approximate: one assistant reviewer agreed on 54/54 binary decisions and 52/54 four-class acts in a targeted blind review. No tools were executed. Intervals resample source clusters or authored families conditional on two fitted seeds. They do not measure training-run uncertainty, establish that 25% is optimal, or establish equivalence in exact recall. ### Changes recommended for the paper

1. Use the 300-prompt table and expanded trade-off figure in the abstract and primary results. Report 272 clusters, the original 72 as nested and previously examined, and the other 228 as a sensitivity analysis. Keep the training design, seeds and loss curves as reported.
2. Quantify the 25%-to-50% contrast in both errors. Update the exact-recall interpretation: balanced training reduced exact recall by 10.5 points versus the base [3.1, 18.2]; the 25% contrast remains uncertain. Give the 100-case denominator and the 96 schema-valid references.
3. Replace the unmatched 367-versus-72 comparison in the main text with the same-question 99.0% versus 67.0% result. Include the 47.0% versus 10.0% false-call comparison and the two directions of discordance. Keep the earlier unmatched experiment in the appendix as historical context.
4. Add the direct-answer control as a separate exploratory result. Distinguish seeking missing information from recognizing that the user has already supplied the requested fact. Keep its percentages separate from When2Call and limit the claim to these authored lookup families.
5. State the local classifier's blinded review results, the pointwise conditional uncertainty, and the limits of two training seeds. Use “requests a call” for generated behavior and “selects the call candidate” for MCQ. Neither procedure executes the tool.
